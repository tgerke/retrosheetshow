# Test fixtures

`chadwick-people-excerpt.csv` holds seven rows copied unchanged from the
`data/people-*.csv` files of the Chadwick Baseball Bureau Register, as of
commit 781c175 (2 October 2026). Contains information from the
[Chadwick Baseball Bureau Register](https://github.com/chadwickbureau/register),
which is made available under the
[ODC Attribution License](http://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1.0/).

from them. The information used here was obtained free of charge from and is
copyrighted by Retrosheet. Interested parties may contact Retrosheet at 20
Sunset Rd., Newark, DE 19711.
